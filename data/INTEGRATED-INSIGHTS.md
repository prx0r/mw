## Key Insight: "No Work Goes to Waste"

A single WorkerRun can produce:

```text
primary outcome (the thing you were paid for)
+ skill (reusable procedure)
+ code asset (reusable component)
+ dataset (exhaust becomes sellable asset)
+ evaluation corpus (training data for future)
```

The dataset may itself become sellable on Mozilla Data Collective or similar. That's the compounding loop.
