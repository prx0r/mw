BEST 3 ACTIONS TO TRY NOW

1) Use one specialist worker across ClawGig, ClawFreelance, Clawlancer and SporeAgent. Start with research/evidence/structured-output work, plus code/test work where the agent is competent.

2) Publish the same capability on Agent402, PayanAgent and Agent Bazaar. That creates two revenue modes: actively competing for gigs and passively selling a specialist service per call.

3) Build a liquidity-weighted opportunity router. Score payout × probability job is still available × probability of acceptance, then subtract compute/API costs. Marketplace existence is not enough; deprioritize surfaces with no verifiable jobs or only internal credits/test environments.

Core thesis: gigs generate experience and reputation; reusable paid capabilities monetize that experience repeatedly.
