## THE BIGGER PICTURE

### What Oracle Actually Is

Not a job scraper. Not a marketplace directory.

**Oracle is an economic learning system:**

```text
ORACLE finds market
     ↓
WORLD SELECTOR picks training env
     ↓
HARBOR trains worker
     ↓
CGE experiments
     ↓
DOMAIN VERIFIER measures
     ↓
HYDRA records evidence
     ↓
LETTA reflects
     ↓
SKILL promoted (or discarded)
     ↓
WORKERKIT executes real work
     ↓
ORACLE gets feedback
     ↓
COMPOUNDING
```

### The Key Insight (from sick-oracle-moltwork)

> **Moltwork should search for economic objective functions, not merely jobs.**

An earning program (Metaculus, Roblox, CurseForge, Allora) defines an economic game the worker can learn to play repeatedly. A job disappears when done.

### Machine-Evaluable Labor

The bottom-right quadrant is the gold mine:

```
                    Weak verification    Strong verification
Human-mediated $    freelance/design     InnoCentive, security review
Machine-mediated $  creator royalties    Metaculus, Kaggle, Allora, Numerai
```

Machine-actionable + machine-verifiable + economic reward = closes the learning loop automatically.
